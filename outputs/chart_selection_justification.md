# Chart Selection Justification

## Overview

This document explains the rationale behind selecting different chart types for the Executive Sales Dashboard. Each visualization was chosen based on the nature of the data and the business question it answers. The selected charts improve readability, support effective decision-making, and provide meaningful insights for business executives.

---

# 1. Sales Trend View

## Chart Type

**Line Chart**

## Business Objective

To analyze sales performance over time and identify trends, seasonality, and business growth.

## Justification

A line chart is the most suitable visualization for time-series data because it clearly displays trends and patterns across different time periods. It enables decision-makers to quickly identify periods of growth or decline and supports forecasting and strategic planning.

---

# 2. Regional Performance View

## Chart Type

**Horizontal Bar Chart**

## Business Objective

To compare sales performance across different regions.

## Justification

A horizontal bar chart allows easy comparison between multiple regions. It clearly highlights the highest and lowest performing regions while maintaining readability, especially when category names are longer.

---

# 3. Category Profitability View

## Chart Type

**Bar Chart**

## Business Objective

To compare profit generated by different product categories and sub-categories.

## Justification

Bar charts provide accurate comparison of numerical values across categories. They make it easy to identify the most profitable and least profitable product categories, helping management prioritize inventory and sales strategies.

---

# 4. Customer Segment Analysis

## Chart Type

**Bar Chart**

## Business Objective

To compare revenue contribution from different customer segments.

## Justification

Customer segments represent discrete categories. A bar chart is the most effective way to compare their sales performance and identify which customer group contributes the highest revenue.

---

# 5. Shipping Performance

## Chart Type

**Bar Chart**

## Business Objective

To evaluate delivery efficiency across different shipping modes.

## Justification

Bar charts allow quick comparison of average delivery days among shipping methods. This helps identify the most efficient shipping option and areas where logistics performance can be improved.

---

# 6. Discount vs Profit Analysis

## Chart Type

**Scatter Plot**

## Business Objective

To analyze the relationship between discount percentage and profit.

## Justification

A scatter plot is ideal for exploring relationships between two continuous numerical variables. It helps identify whether increasing discounts leads to higher or lower profitability and reveals possible outliers.

---

# 7. Return Analysis

## Chart Type

**Bar Chart**

## Business Objective

To compare product returns across different categories.

## Justification

Bar charts effectively compare return counts among categories, making it easy to identify products with higher return rates and investigate potential quality or service issues.

---

# KPI Cards

The dashboard includes KPI cards to provide an instant summary of business performance.

The selected KPIs are:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Average Order Value
* Return Rate

These KPIs provide executives with a quick overview of organizational performance without requiring detailed analysis.

---

# Interactive Filters

The following filters are included to improve dashboard usability:

* Region
* Category
* Customer Segment
* Order Date
* Ship Mode
* Campaign Channel

Interactive filters enable users to focus on specific business scenarios and perform customized analysis.

---

# Visualization Design Principles

The following principles were considered while selecting chart types:

* Appropriate visualization for each business question.
* Consistent color palette throughout the dashboard.
* Clear titles and labels.
* Minimal visual clutter.
* Easy comparison of values.
* Interactive user experience.
* Executive-friendly dashboard layout.
* Accurate representation of business metrics.

---

# Conclusion

The selected visualizations provide a balanced combination of trend analysis, categorical comparison, relationship analysis, and operational monitoring. Together, these charts create an executive dashboard that is easy to understand, visually consistent, and capable of supporting data-driven business decisions.
